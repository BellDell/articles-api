## What to Expect

This is a collaborative coding session where we'll work together to build a simple API. We're primarily interested in seeing how you approach problems, communicate your thinking, and collaborate in real-time - not in having you prepare anything beforehand.

## AI Usage Guidelines

Please try to avoid using AI assistants (ChatGPT, Copilot, etc.) during the session. We want to see your natural problem-solving process and how you think through challenges. However, it's absolutely fine to:
- Ask us questions
- Look up documentation online
- Search for specific syntax or examples on Google
- Reference Stack Overflow or similar for quick solutions

## Technical Requirements

Please ensure you have the following ready:

- **Development Environment:** Python 3.12+ (with pip) OR Node.js LTS
- **IDE/Editor:** Your preferred code editor (VS Code, PyCharm, etc.)
- **Zoom:** Installed with screen sharing permissions enabled
- **Environment:** Quiet space with stable internet connection
